Perspective QR

A QR graphic mounted on a hip-roof solid — it only resolves into a clean, scannable square when viewed from directly overhead, at the chosen scan distance above the ridge.

Graphic coverage
Base length L cm
Base width W cm
Ridge length R cm
Height H cm
Scan distance (above ridge) cm

File format
Print paper size

Pieces larger than one sheet are tiled across multiple pages with cut guides and page labels — print at 100% / actual size (turn off "fit to page" in your print dialog), then trim and tape the tiles together.

One true-scale file per piece (the trapezoid graphic, plus two triangles in 4-face mode), each labeled with its dimensions.

A single .3mf file with the solid as one part and the QR modules as a second part, embedded 5mm into the surface — one color per part. In PrusaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/Bambu Studio, the model loads as one object with two parts already in place; just assign each part its own filament/color for a multi-material print.

How this works

The solid is a hip roof: a rectangular base (L×W) topped by a shorter ridge (R) at height H. R=0 gives a pyramid; R=L gives a plain gable-roof prism. Each sloped face is pre-warped for a camera positioned the chosen scan distance above the ridge top, looking straight down — e.g. the realistic distance someone would hold a phone to scan it — so the whole graphic reassembles into a clean, scannable square only from that vantage point. From any other angle, or a different distance, it looks bent across the ridge.
